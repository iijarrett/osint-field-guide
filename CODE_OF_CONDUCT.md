# Code of conduct

This project is a reference for people who investigate and defend. We want contributors of every background and
experience level to feel able to take part.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be precise and cite your sources, especially for claims about tools, prices, laws and people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Keep discussion on the guide. Questions about how to investigate a particular person are out of scope here.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Using issues or pull requests to target, expose or collect information about a private individual.
- Requests for help to stalk, dox, harass or gain unauthorized access to anything. These are closed and may be reported.
- Promoting a product you profit from without saying so.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block
accounts that do so repeatedly. To report a problem, email security@jarrettd.com with a link to the content.
Reports are handled privately.
